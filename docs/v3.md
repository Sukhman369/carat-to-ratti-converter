## 1. Real-World Commercial Problem: Price per Ratti vs Price per Carat

### 📌 The Customer Dilemma & Trade Confusion
In India, the Middle East, and South Asia:
* Astrologers prescribe gemstone weight in **Ratti** (*"Wear a 5.25 Ratti Pukhraj"*).
* Jewelers often quote price **per Ratti** (*"₹8,000 per Ratti"*).
* Certified Gemological Laboratories (GIA, IGI, IIGJ) issue certificates strictly in **Metric Carats** (*4.78 Carats*).

### 💡 The Solution: Optional Gemstone Price & Cost Estimator
A clean, collapsible or toggleable price module integrated into the calculator:
1. **User Inputs:**
   * Unit Price in ₹ / $ / AED (e.g. `₹8,000`).
   * Price Basis: `Per Ratti` OR `Per Carat`.
2. **Instant Calculated Outputs:**
   * **Total Gemstone Cost:** `Weight × Unit Rate`.
   * **Effective Counter Rate:** If price entered is per Ratti, shows effective price per Carat (`Price ÷ 0.91`), preventing buyers from overpaying.
   * **Pakka vs Sunee Price Impact:** Shows the financial difference between buying on certified `0.91` Pakka ratio vs traditional `0.90` Sunee ratio.

## 2. Smart Astrological Guidance & Astrological Safety Checks

### ⚠️ A. Inauspicious "Paune" (+0.75) Warning Logic
* **Trigger:** User inputs an inauspicious fraction ending in `0.75` (or within $\pm 0.05$ of a Paune weight, such as `4.75`, `6.75`, `7.75`).
* **UI Behavior:**
  * Displays a soft, polite amber/rose advisory banner.
  * **Advisory Message:**
    > *"⚠️ Paune (e.g., 4.75 Ratti) is an incomplete fraction traditionally avoided in Vedic astrology. Consider 5.25 Ratti (Sawa Paanch / 4.78 Ct) for positive growth and auspicious results."*
  * Includes a 1-click **"Upgrade to Nearest Sawa"** button.

### 🌟 B. Nearest Auspicious Sawa Recommender
* **Trigger:** User enters an arbitrary decimal (e.g., `5.10 Ratti`, `4.60 Carats`).
* **UI Behavior:**
  * Displays a small pill underneath the result:
    > *"👉 Nearest Auspicious Vedic Weight: **5.25 Ratti (Sawa Paanch / 4.78 Ct)** [Load Weight]"*

## 4. Edge-Case & Usability Engineering

1. **Graceful Zero & Empty States:**
   * When user clears the input via backspace, keep UI stable with `0.00` placeholder without showing `NaN`.
2. **Fast Keyboard Steppers:**
   * Support `+` and `-` incremental steppers ($\pm 0.25$ step size) for easy adjustment without full re-typing.
3. **WhatsApp Gem Trade Formatter:**
   * Formatted clipboard / WhatsApp share message tailored for jeweler-client quotes:
     ```text
     💎 Gemstone Weight & Astrological Details:
     • Weight in Ratti (Pakka): 5.25 Rt (सवा पांच)
     • Weight in Carats: 4.78 Ct
     • Weight in Milligrams: 955.5 mg
     • Astrological Status: Auspicious Sawa (Jupiter / Guru)
     • Verified on: rattitocarat.com
     ```
4. **Body Weight Edge Boundaries:**
   * Minimum threshold: `30 kg`
   * Maximum threshold: `150 kg`
   * Pediatric advisory for body weights $< 35\text{ kg}$ recommending natural pearls or light substitutes under astrologer guidance.
